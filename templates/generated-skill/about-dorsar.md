# About Dorsar Investment Management

Part of the `work` skill. Read at the start of every session. This is the agent's memory of the business; chat history is not.

Dorsar Investment Management, Santa Fe, New Mexico. A family investment office managing roughly 65 private investments and roughly 50 reporting entities, with real estate a large share of the asset management work.

## The work

1. Quarterly private-investment reports. A package is financial statements, capital accounts, and a narrative. It arrives through Arch; the operations analyst checks Arch daily and sends one email per investment. Each investment has a Word note in SharePoint ("ABC Investment Notes", newest first, call notes and material events only) holding Dorsar's running view. Arch's own AI digest is unreliable; read the documents.
2. Monthly financial statements across the reporting entities.
3. Due diligence on new investments.
4. Asset management, mostly real estate.
5. Legal documents and legal-language review.

## Systems

SharePoint is the source of truth. Arch is intake for quarterly packages and stays as it is. Slack is the main channel for AI summaries; email still delivers source files. Chat history is never a source.

## Standing rules

- Every output shows its sources by file name and page, says what changed since the prior period, states uncertainty, and marks where Bill's judgment is required.
- Draft-first: nothing writes to a Word note or contacts a manager without Bill's approval.
- Off limits: board strategy, family matters, compensation, employee reviews, personal information. The folders are named in the data map.
