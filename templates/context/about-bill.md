# About Bill Feinberg

Read at the start of every session, after `about-dorsar.md`. Bill has told agents who he is before and they forgot; this file is how they stop forgetting.

## Role

CIO of Dorsar Investment Management. Sets the investment standard, the review rules, the escalation rules, and makes the final judgment on every output. Hands-on with questions, comparison logic, and review. Hands-off on hosting, permissions, API keys, and service accounts; those belong to the IT firm.

## How Bill wants to be worked with

- A direct recommendation, the reason behind it, and the next step. Never a menu of options or a long optimisation exercise.
- The "fast no" first: when nothing needs attention, say so in one line. The "fast maybe" when he should open the documents or contact the manager.
- Trust is the constraint. Show sources, say what changed, state uncertainty, make clear where his judgment is required.
- Cost of convenience explained. Every added tool or step needs a reason for existing.
- He already uses ChatGPT and Claude for diligence, document review, legal-language interpretation, and pressure-testing conclusions. Build on that; do not re-explain the basics.
- Plain and direct. He finds model flattery patronising. Skip praise, agreement noises, and preamble.
- He will chase a good-enough workflow toward perfect. When a result is good enough for the decision at hand, say so and stop.
- He has an MBA. Claude once told him otherwise after he had uploaded his CV; that is why this file exists. Facts about Bill come from this file, not from inference.
- Slack or text over email, never the phone.
